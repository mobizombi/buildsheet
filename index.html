What a schedule looks like

Every sheet ends with a plan worked backwards from your con date, so the slow parts (paint cure, wig styling, test wear) get their time before the panic week. Here is the The Mandalorian plan:

The Mandalorian - 8-week plan80-140 hours total
DueWork
8 weeks outOrder helmet files or kit, foam, cement, paints. Tape-pattern the armor.
7 weeks outPrint or assemble helmet. Start filling and sanding seams.
6 weeks outCut and heat-form all armor plates. Dry-fit on your body.
5 weeks outSeal armor and helmet. Glossy black base coat, 24h cure.
4 weeks outChrome coats on everything. Build gauntlets and blaster.
3 weeks outSoft goods: flight suit, cape, belt and pouches.
2 weeks outStrapping and mounting - attach armor to the suit. Full fitting.
Con weekTest wear for 2 hours, weathering pass, pack a repair kit.

Questions

Is this just a chatbot answer?

No. A sheet is structured: every part has materials, a cost range and hours, and the schedule is built from your real date and weekly hours. It is a plan you can print and tick off.

Does it work for original characters?

Yes - references are all it needs. OCs and mash-ups work the same as any other costume.

What if my con is only three weeks away?

The sheet will tell you honestly what fits in three weeks and what to buy instead of build.
