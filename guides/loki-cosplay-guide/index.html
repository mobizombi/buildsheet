How to make a Loki cosplay

Loki is half tailoring, half armor. The coat is the longest job, the horned helmet is the most fun, and the gold has to look like metal rather than paint - plan a full week for finishing.

Techniques by part

Coat panels

Start from a commercial long-coat pattern and swap in brocade panels. Interface the leather-look panels so they hang straight.

Helmet horns

Build each horn from stacked foam wedges, carve with a sharp blade, sand with a rotary tool, then seal and fill the seams.

Gold that reads as metal

Gold over a black base, then a thin black wash wiped back into the recesses. Flat gold looks like plastic in photos.

Cape hang

Attach the cape under the shoulder armor with snaps, and add a weighted hem so it swings rather than flaps.

The horns make doorways and crowds tricky. Build the helmet to sit snug with foam padding inside so it does not tip forward.

7-week build schedule

Worked backwards from con day. If you are starting later, cut the slow-cure steps first and buy rather than build the smaller parts.
