How to make a Geralt of Rivia cosplay

Geralt is a leather-and-straps build. The jacket is the biggest job, the wig is the biggest risk, and the two swords on the back are what makes him readable from across a hall.

Techniques by part

Faux-leather armor

Quilt channels into vinyl over thin batting with a long stitch. Glue foam behind the shoulder and chest pieces to give them weight.

Sword props

Core in foam around a light dowel, seal, then silver-leaf or metallic paint. Keep both under your con weight and length limits.

Wig styling

Thin the hairline slightly and set a loose half-up tie. Dull the shine with dry shampoo - shiny white reads as nylon.

Scar

Build the eye scar in thin layers of scar wax, blend edges with a stipple sponge, and set with powder.

Coloured contacts must come from a licensed source with a fitting - never buy novelty lenses from a market stall. Plenty of great Geralts skip them.
