How to make a Wonder Woman cosplay

This is an armor build that has to be comfortable for a full day. The bodice fit decides everything - pattern it on your body, not a mannequin, and leave time for the gold and red paint to cure.

Techniques by part

Bodice patterning

Duct-tape pattern over a fitted top while wearing it, mark the emblem and seams, then transfer to foam in panels.

Flexible paint

Seal foam with a flexible primer before metallics. Rigid primers crack where the bodice bends when you move.

Boot covers

Pattern covers over the actual boots you will wear, with a zip at the back. Comfortable soles matter more than heel height.

Shield straps

Use two straps and a handle, mounted with plates on the back so the shield sits on your forearm without twisting.

Put a zip or hook-and-loop closure at the back of the bodice. Lacing looks great and takes 20 minutes with a helper.

6-week build schedule

Worked backwards from con day. If you are starting later, cut the slow-cure steps first and buy rather than build the smaller parts.
