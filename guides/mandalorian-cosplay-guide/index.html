How to make a Mandalorian cosplay

Mando is a hard-surface build: most of the work is the helmet and the chrome armor, and most first-timers lose time on paint. Start the helmet first and give the chrome finish a full week - it is the part people photograph.

Techniques by part

Patterning armor

Wrap yourself (or a friend) in plastic wrap and tape, draw the plates on, cut it off and trace onto foam. Fit beats accuracy every time.

Heat forming foam

Heat EVA until it is warm to the touch, then curve it over your leg or a bucket. Kerf-cut the back of thick foam for tight curves.

Chrome that looks like metal

Seal the foam, lay a glossy black base, let it cure 24h, then mist mirror-chrome in thin coats. Chrome over a dull base looks grey.

Weathering

Dry-brush dark brown and black into edges and wipe back. Mando is lived-in - clean armor reads as a costume.

Mount armor with elastic and snaps, not glue straight to the suit - you will need to sit, and to take it off for the bathroom.

8-week build schedule

Worked backwards from con day. If you are starting later, cut the slow-cure steps first and buy rather than build the smaller parts.
