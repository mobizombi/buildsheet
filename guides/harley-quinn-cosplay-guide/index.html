Techniques by part

Wig styling

Steam-set the pigtails on a wig head, then trim bangs a little at a time. Use got2b-style spray to hold the volume through a long day.

Vinyl lettering

Cut heat-transfer vinyl mirrored, weed it, press with a cloth over it. Test on scrap first so the tee does not scorch.

Distressed jacket

Sand the edges lightly and add iron-on patches. A little wear reads as character, too much reads as cheap.

Makeup

Do a full trial run a week before. Set with powder and setting spray so it survives photos and heat.

Check your convention prop rules - many require bats to be foam or hollow. Bring the policy page on your phone.
