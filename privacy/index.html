Privacy

If you join the waitlist we collect your email address, and optionally the costume you are building, your con date and the page you signed up from. We use it only to tell you when BuildSheet opens and to plan which guides to write next.

Signups are delivered to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service. We never sell or share your address. You can ask us to delete it at any time by replying to any email from us.

The cost calculator runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing.
